# MUMOSA Team Alignment Addendum

This addendum captures the updated team direction from the latest board screenshots and should be treated as the short decision record for the digital prototype.

## Purpose

Align the team on who the prototype is for, what MUMOSA currently is, what the future vision can become, and what the Axure RP 11 prototype should prove this semester.

## Current Client Reality

MUMOSA is currently best framed as a multimodal situational-awareness dashboard for forensic and post-crisis investigation. It combines text, images, schema graphs, simulations, and source-grounded question answering.

The published system direction supports investigation and training. It is not yet a fully deployed real-time field response platform.

Core current capabilities:

- Forensic and post-crisis analysis.
- Investigation and responder training.
- Text, image, schema, and simulation evidence.
- Source-grounded Q/A so users can trace AI claims back to evidence.

## Future Client Direction

The future vision can include more live and agent-like support, but those should be presented as a roadmap rather than the full semester prototype.

Future-facing capabilities:

- Real-time response support.
- Dynamic timeline and time-slider views.
- Danger or hazard highlighting.
- Scenario planning and what-if analysis.
- Agent-like guidance that recommends next investigative steps.

## Recommended Prototype Focus

The prototype should focus on one main user, one main task, one incident scenario, and one VR interaction worth demonstrating.

Primary user:

- Post-crisis investigator, CSI, fire investigator, or similar analyst.

Primary task:

- Reconstruct what happened and verify evidence across multiple modalities.

Dashboard role:

- Search.
- Source comparison.
- Chronology.
- Evidence browsing.
- Event overview.

VR role:

- Spatial evidence review.
- Hazard and scene context.
- Training support.
- Optional deeper inspection of a complex physical scene.

Key screens:

- Incident entry.
- Event overview.
- Evidence review.
- Timeline and schema/event map.
- Optional VR scene review handoff.

## What To Avoid

The project should not overbuild beyond what can be explained, tested, and refined this semester.

Avoid:

- Making professional role filters a hard gate before users enter the system.
- Designing separate full journeys for every profession.
- Attempting a full live real-time sensor fusion platform.
- Letting VR replace the dashboard.
- Presenting VR as the whole product instead of a specialized support mode.

## Decision Statement

The clearest direction is a web-first MUMOSA dashboard for post-crisis investigation, supported by an optional VR/spatial mode when the investigator needs to understand the physical scene, hazards, or evidence relationships in place.
